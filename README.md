## Core Knowledge Breakdown

### 1. Why Card Categories & US-Issued BINs Matter
Different online platforms and payment flows have different requirements depending on the card category. **CoreGold provides US-issued card BINs** grouped by practical scenarios such as AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Grok), Apple Pay / Google Pay, Google Play / Apple Store, and overseas e-commerce, ads, and cloud services. Choosing the card category that matches your actual use case is a clean starting point.

### 2. AVS (Billing Address) Consistency
When issuing a card on CoreGold, you can keep **Auto-Generate Billing Info** checked or uncheck it to enter Name, State, City, Street Address, and ZIP Code manually.
- **Key rule**: Keep the billing address you enter at merchant checkout consistent with the billing address recorded on your card. Mismatched details are a common reason checkout forms fail verification.

### 3. Card Management, Balance Control & Credential Safety
Inside **Card Management**, you can inspect your card number, expiration date, CVV, available balance, billing address, and status, and perform top-ups, withdrawals, or freezes as needed.
- Always keep your full card number, expiration date, CVV, and verification codes private—never share them with others.

### 4. TRON (TRC20) Deposits & Wallet Verification Codes
- **Funding with USDT**: If you don't have USDT yet, you can buy it on platforms such as OKX or Binance, then transfer it via the **TRON (TRC20)** network. Double-check the address, network, and amount before sending; transfers credit automatically (contact support if not credited within 10 minutes).
- **Where to find the wallet verification code**: All CoreGold card BINs can be bound to **Apple Pay** or **Google Pay**. After manually entering the card number, expiration date, and CVV in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet and choosing **email verification**, return to CoreGold **Card Management**, open the card details page, and tap **Add to Digital Wallet (添加到数字钱包)** at the bottom to view the code, then enter it back into your wallet app.

> **Card Tip**: Register your CoreGold account ahead of time and test a small deposit and card setup so you already know the flow when a new tool or subscription needs payment.

## Simple Step-by-Step Setup Tutorial

### Step 1: Register on CoreGold
Visit [https://app.coregold.io/signup?invite_code=ADvlj9b](https://app.coregold.io/signup?invite_code=ADvlj9b), register using your regular email address, and log in to the user dashboard.

### Step 2: Deposit USDT via TRON (TRC20)
1. In the left menu, click **My Wallet (我的钱包)** -> **Deposit (充值)**.
2. Enter your deposit amount to display the USDT deposit address and QR code.
3. Open your crypto wallet or exchange (such as OKX or Binance), select USDT withdrawal/transfer, and choose the **TRON (TRC20)** network.
4. Copy the deposit address or scan the QR code, verify the address, network, and amount, and confirm the transfer. Funds credit automatically after transfer (contact customer support if not credited within 10 minutes).

### Step 3: Select Plan, Card Category & Billing Info
1. Click **Apply for Card (申请开卡)** in the left menu and choose an **Individual Plan** (such as Diamond Plan or AI Subscription) or **Team Plan** (refer to live page details for pricing, supported scenarios, and fees).
2. Pick the matching card category (CoreGold provides US-issued card BINs) and enter the initial deposit amount.
3. Check **Auto-Generate Billing Info** or uncheck it to fill in Name, State, City, Street Address, and ZIP Code manually, keeping billing addresses consistent during merchant checkout.

### Step 4: View Card Details & Bind Apple Pay / Google Pay
1. Open **Card Management (卡片管理)** and select your card to view card number, expiration date, CVV, available balance, billing address, and status, or use top-up, withdrawal, and freeze controls.
2. To bind **Apple Pay** or **Google Pay**:
   - Open **Apple Wallet** on iPhone or **Google Wallet** on Android, select **Add Payment Card**, and enter the card number, expiration date, and CVV manually.
   - Choose **email verification** when prompted.
   - Return to CoreGold **Card Management**, open the card details page, and click **Add to Digital Wallet (添加到数字钱包)** at the bottom to view the verification code.
   - Enter the verification code back into your mobile wallet to finish binding.
